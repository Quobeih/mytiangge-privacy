MyTiangge Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026 · Developer: MyTiangge · App: com.mytiangge.app

In short

  • Your store records (sales, products, stock, utang, customers) are saved only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • Your password is saved only on your phone, in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • The app works without internet. It has no ads, no sign-in to our servers, and no tracking by us.
  • The barcode scanner is provided by Google and sends Google limited technical data so the scanner works (details below).

1. Who we are

MyTiangge is an offline record book for sari-sari stores and small sellers in the Philippines. "We" means the MyTiangge developer. You can contact us at rotsensuerte@gmail.com (rotsensuerte@gmail.com).

2. Information you enter in the app

You may enter your store name, your name as the owner, products and prices, stock counts, sales, and customer names, phone numbers, notes, utang and payments. This information is stored in a private database inside the app on your phone. We do not collect it, see it, sell it or share it.

If you enter a customer's name or phone number, you are responsible for using it only for your store's own records.

3. Your account and password

The account you create in MyTiangge exists only on your phone. Your password and recovery code are never stored as plain text: they are saved as salted PBKDF2 hashes in the app's private storage, and they are never sent to us or anyone else.

4. Barcode scanner (Google)

When you use the barcode scanner, MyTiangge uses the Google code scanner from Google Play services (ML Kit). The camera image is processed on your phone, and MyTiangge itself does not have camera permission. According to Google, ML Kit collects device information, app information, performance metrics and error codes for diagnostics and usage analytics, encrypts this data in transit, and does not share it with third parties. When auto-zoom is used, it also collects a temporary scanning-session ID and zoom changes. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Backups and exports

FeatureWhat happens
Back up data / Export sales (CSV)Creates a file in a place you choose (phone storage, SD card, Google Drive app, etc.). The file contains your records; keep it private.
Restore from backupReads a backup file you choose and replaces the records on your phone.
Android automatic backupIf Google backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data to your own Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. This is controlled by you and Google, not by us.

6. Permissions

MyTiangge does not request permission to your camera, contacts, location, microphone or files. Files are opened or saved only when you pick them yourself.

7. Children

MyTiangge is a business tool for adult store owners. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Your records stay on your phone until you delete them in the app or uninstall MyTiangge. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes everything stored by the app on that phone. Backup files you created must be deleted by you. Because we hold no data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf. You can also delete your account and all records in the app: Settings → Delete account and all data. Full steps: Delete your MyTiangge account.

9. Security

Records are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Sensitive actions such as voiding a sale, recording a payment, deleting records, and backing up, restoring or exporting data require your password. Please protect your phone with a screen lock as well.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and publish the new version at this same address.
