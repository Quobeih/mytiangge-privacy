Delete your MyTiangge account and data

App: MyTiangge: Sari-sari Listahan (com.mytiangge.app) · Developer: MyTiangge

MyTiangge keeps your account and store records only on your phone. The developer has no server and holds no copy of your account or records, so you delete them yourself on the phone, in a few taps.

How to delete your account (in the app)

  1. Open MyTiangge and log in.
  2. Tap Settings (bottom bar).
  3. Under Data & backup, tap Delete account and all data.
  4. Read the warning and tap Delete everything.
  5. Confirm with your password or fingerprint. Your account and all records are deleted right away, and the app returns to the Welcome screen.

If you can no longer open the app

Uninstall MyTiangge, or go to your phone's Settings → Apps → MyTiangge → Storage → Clear data. This deletes the account and every record stored by the app on that phone.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Account: owner name, password (hashed), recovery code, fingerprint settingDeleted immediately
Store records: products, stock, sales, customers, utang, paymentsDeleted immediately
Settings: store name, auto-lock and other preferencesDeleted immediately
Backup or CSV files you saved yourselfKept. They are your files; delete them from wherever you saved them (phone storage, Google Drive, etc.).
Android automatic backup in your Google account (only if Google backup is on)Kept by Google until it is replaced or removed. Delete it in Google Drive → Backups, or turn off backup in your phone's Settings → Google → Backup.
Technical diagnostics from Google's barcode scanner (ML Kit)Held by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. It is not linked to your MyTiangge account, and the developer cannot access it.

The developer keeps no data after deletion, because the developer never receives your data. There is no additional retention period.

Paano burahin ang account (Filipino)

  1. Buksan ang MyTiangge at mag-log in.
  2. I-tap ang Settings.
  3. Sa Data at backup, i-tap ang Burahin ang account at lahat ng data.
  4. I-tap ang Burahin lahat, saka kumpirmahin gamit ang password o fingerprint.

Agad na mabubura ang account at lahat ng tala sa phone. Kung hindi mabuksan ang app, i-uninstall ito o i-clear ang data nito sa Settings ng phone. Hindi mabubura ang mga backup file na ikaw mismo ang nag-save. Para sa tanong: rotsensuerte@gmail.com
